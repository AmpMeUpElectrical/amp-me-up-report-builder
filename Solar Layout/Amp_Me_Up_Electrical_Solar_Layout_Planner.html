Job set-up

Everything is measured in millimetres. Measure the roof face capping edge to capping edge — not by counting whole tiles, because the edge tile usually overlaps a large part of the capping and counting can cost you a panel.

Roof & site

The certification defines h as the average roof height above ground (Note 9, Figure 1) — not the gutter and not the ridge, but halfway between, so it does include the roof. Measure both and the planner takes the average. b and d are the building's plan dimensions at the walls. s is the gap under the panel — the certification bans a panel within 2s of any edge or the ridge, so the setback used is whichever is larger of 200 mm and 2s.

Roof face

Tile

Tile hooks must be fixed to the truss top chord, so the across-slope fixing positions are the trusses. They run up the slope, so their spacing is measured across the roof.

Exposed length is the up-slope distance from one row of tiles to the next. The hook shift limit is 60 mm for a standard interface (120 mm between the furthest screw holes). On a profiled tile the hook is always screwed to a truss, then slid along until it lands in a trough.

Tin

Thin steel derates the certified spacing hard — 0.55 mm is 27% of the table value.

Panel & layout

Spacings read from Gamcorp certification 14022-MA PRO-F1 (MA PRO Rail, tin & tile, AS/NZS 1170.2:2021), valid to 30/11/2027. Clamp zones from the panel manufacturers' own installation manuals. This tool assists set-out; it does not replace the certification, the panel manual, or an assessment of the roof structure.
